Hive Sort

Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · updated September 30, 2026

Hive Sort is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. It has no accounts and no analytics of its own. This policy explains what the app stores and what the ad and purchase services it uses collect.

HomeSupport

What we collect

We, the developer, collect nothing. The app never sends your data to us. Your progress (levels, scores, coins, settings) is saved only on your device, and deleting the app deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Hive Sort is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: banners, an occasional ad between games, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software collects some data directly from your device:

  • device identifiers and your IP address (which indicates your approximate region),
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps),
  • diagnostic data such as crash and performance information from the ad software.

Your choices. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, UK and some US states), the app first shows Google's consent form. On iOS, it then asks for permission to track. If you allow tracking, Google may use your device's advertising identifier to show ads based on your interests. If you decline, you'll see non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Declining never limits the game.

This data goes to Google, not to us, and is handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that turns off banners and between-game ads.

Purchases (Apple)

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, and the app sends no purchase data to us or to anyone else. Your purchases belong to your Apple ID, which is how Restore purchases brings them back. Apple handles this data under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

Hive Sort is rated 4+ but is not designed for or directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Because we don't hold any of your data, there's nothing for us to access or delete. To exercise your rights over data held by Google or Apple, use the links above. You can reset your device's advertising identifier or turn off tracking in iOS Settings at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

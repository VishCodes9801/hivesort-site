How to play

Drag a stack from the tray onto an empty hex. Matching top colors next to each other slide together. When one color reaches ten, it turns into honey and clears. The game ends when the tray has no room left on the board.

Frequently asked

My purchases are missing

Open Settings (the gear on the home screen), then tap Restore purchases. Remove Ads and the Honey Bundle come back on any device signed in with the same Apple ID. Coins are consumable and can't be restored.

How do I stop ads?

Buy Remove Ads once from the Shop. It removes banners and between-level ads. You can still choose to watch a rewarded ad for a bonus.

Can I change my ad-tracking choice?

Yes. Go to iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and switch Hive Sort off or on. Declining never affects gameplay.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress. Purchases can be restored.

Too much motion or hard to tell colors apart?

Settings has Reduced motion, Colorblind symbols, and separate Sound, Music and Haptics switches.
